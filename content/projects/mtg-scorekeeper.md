I wanted a Magic: The Gathering tracker that I could change whenever a new idea came up, so I built one. It handles life totals, poison, commander damage, and Commander search through Scryfall. The layout works on phones and larger shared screens, which makes it easy to leave in the middle of the table during a game.

This was my hands-on introduction to the modern front-end stack. I learned React and Vite by making something my friends and I could actually use, then kept adding the details that generic counter apps did not handle the way I wanted.

There is no account and no personal data to manage. Open the app, set up the players, and start the game.
