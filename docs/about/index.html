At work

I wear two hats at WeSuite: Lead Scrum Master and Head of Quality Assurance. I work across desktop, web, and database products, which means my day can move from testing a release to sorting out a backlog to getting several teams aligned on what actually needs to ship.

Recently I owned quality for a major desktop-to-web rebuild. I tested the new product against the one our customers already knew, followed defects through resolution, prepared the release notes, and helped coordinate the handoff. Around the same time, I led our move to a simpler work-management system and used the migration as a chance to clear out years of backlog clutter.

After work

The home lab is where I get to be the developer, operator, tester, and occasionally the person who broke it. I also build small apps when there is something specific I want to use. Away from a keyboard, I am usually climbing, hiking, looking through a telescope, or making something in the kitchen that takes longer than it should.
